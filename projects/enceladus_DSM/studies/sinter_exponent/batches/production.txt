# WHY BOTH ARMS
# -------------
# alpha = 1/3 is the attachment-limited prediction; tau_sub here is 64.7%
# kinetic / 25.3% thermal / 10.7% vapour, and both non-kinetic channels are
# made worse by a sealed box in the same direction -- latent heat accumulates
# with no sink 0.1 mm from the spheres, and the vapour reservoir depletes.
# Both bend the slope DOWNWARD over the run, which is easy to misread as "our
# model gives a smaller exponent".
#
# Neither arm is exactly their apparatus: zero-flux walls 0.1 mm away give the
# heat nowhere to go, while Dirichlet walls 0.1 mm away are an infinite
# reservoir far closer than their real box. The two BRACKET it. Compare the
# EXPONENT, not the rate:
#   agree    -> the boundary treatment is not bending it; quote the sealed arm
#               and make this a one-line robustness statement.
#   differ   -> the Dirichlet arm is the closer analogue of a large box on a
#               cooled stage; quote that one and say so explicitly, rather
#               than presenting the pair as an uncertainty band.
#
# Expected: a local slope of 0.30-0.33 across r/R = 0.09-0.35 -- the
# exact-fillet ideal, NOT a flat 1/3 -- against Demmenie's 0.26-0.33.
#
# 1. sealed (zero-flux), mass-conserving — matches their constant sphere volume
