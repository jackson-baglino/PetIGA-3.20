B. KINETICS: the temperature sweep is confounded with alpha_c
==============================================================================
  beta_sub0 = 1.4e5 was HARDCODED and identical at every temperature.
  The solver forms beta_sub = beta_sub0 / (rho_i/rho_vs(T)), so a
  constant beta_sub0 does NOT mean a constant condensation coefficient.

     T     rho_vs   alpha_c  a_c*rho_vs   eps_max  eps/max  Nx_req  coarse  tau_sub dt_med/tau

   -25  5.197e-04 9.351e-02  4.8592e-05 3.194e-07     2.74    8857     7.8    271.1       4.92
   -30  3.122e-04 1.573e-01  4.9101e-05 1.918e-07     4.57   14747    12.9    380.0       3.51
   -35  1.835e-04 2.703e-01  4.9620e-05 1.128e-07     7.77   25085    22.0    571.1       2.34
   -40  1.055e-04 4.755e-01  5.0154e-05 6.480e-08    13.51   43651    38.2    914.2       1.46

  alpha_c rises 5.672e-02 -> 4.755e-01 (8.4x) as rho_vs falls 8.0x.
  Their product -- which sets the sublimation flux -- is constant to 4.2%
  across the whole 20 C sweep. The sweep therefore CANNOT resolve a
  temperature effect: it was cancelled in the parameter choice.

  Against the anchored Arrhenius alpha_c(T) (1e-1 @ -2 C, 1e-3 @ -40 C;
  Libbrecht 2017, Braun/Fourteau/Lowe 2024; band 1e-4..1e-1):
      T    implied   Arrhenius    ratio  in band
    -20  5.672e-02   1.341e-02      4.2      yes
    -25  9.351e-02   7.289e-03     12.8      yes
    -30  1.573e-01   3.863e-03     40.7       NO
    -35  2.703e-01   1.994e-03    135.6       NO
    -40  4.755e-01   1.000e-03    475.5       NO
  The literature alpha_c FALLS with temperature; the implied one RISES.
  The sign of the T-dependence of the attachment kinetics is backwards.
