\textbf{Framing.} Dry snow metamorphism is the story; the model covers one
part of it, sintering by vapor transport, and the bulk conductivity that
results, under conditions a laboratory can reach. Enceladus is the motivation
and the closing implication. Every claim stays inside the simulated range:
$-40$ to $-5\,^{\circ}$C, air at 1~atm, sealed pores, 50~$\mu$m grains,
30~days, two dimensions.

\textbf{How to read this.} Read against
\texttt{Literature/BaglinoJackson\_DSM\_manuscript.pdf} (27 pages, 657
numbered lines) and the Overleaf project in
\texttt{Literature/BaglinoJackson\_DSM\_manuscript.zip}, which was compiled
for the build check below. Paragraphs are identified by the draft's line numbers
(L\,first--last). \Have covered; \Miss not there; \Stale there but no longer
matches the model or results; \Wrong a statement that is wrong as written;
\Cut can go, or shrink, without loss. Boxes mark where a figure or table
goes. Themes are prompts, not text.

\subsection{What Vapor Transport Alone Reproduces}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The unsaturated pair.} With $\alpha_c$ at the top of its
  range, the vapor route gives 41\% of the measured growth at
  $-20\,^{\circ}$C; the rest needs another route. At $-5\,^{\circ}$C it
  gives more than was measured, and the authors doubt that data set. The two
  cannot both be closed by one $\alpha_c$.
\item \textbf{The saturated pair.} The exponent, against Demmenie's and
  against 1/3. \Open{which reading, as in Section~3.1.}
\item \textbf{$\alpha_c$ is an effective parameter.} Reuse L469--491, the
  survey of Bouvet and Braun, shortened by half. What it means for the
  aggregates: their rates carry the uncertainty of $\alpha_c$; the
  conductivity as a function of SSA does not.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Temperature as a Rescaling of Time}
\begin{enumerate}
\setcounter{enumi}{3}
\item \textbf{Why the collapse holds.} One route, a constant $\alpha_c$,
  transfer limited by attachment: temperature enters through
  $\tau_{\mathrm{sub}}$ alone. In this model it is close to a consequence of
  the construction; say so.
\item \textbf{What would break it.} A temperature-dependent $\alpha_c$; a
  second route with a different activation energy; a temperature gradient.
  The clock's activation energy, about 50~kJ\,mol$^{-1}$, against
  $24.3 \pm 3.3$ from Choukroun et al.: the difference is itself evidence of
  another route in their samples.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Specific Surface Area as the State Variable}
\begin{enumerate}
\setcounter{enumi}{5}
\item \textbf{Density is not enough.} Reuse L498--517 (Calonne 2011, 2014),
  cut to half: conductivity changes at constant density there and here.
\item \textbf{The power law and its limit.} One exponent for $\varphi \le
  0.375$; porosity sets the level. Above that the exponent weakens and
  scatters, and the packings are anisotropic.
\item \textbf{Why packings fan out.} Connectivity, not sampling: the scatter
  in the exponent does not fall with domain size. The two-dimensional point:
  discs at these porosities are close to the limit of a connected packing.
  \Open{the percolation and packing thresholds for this argument are not yet
  checked against a source.}
\end{enumerate}
