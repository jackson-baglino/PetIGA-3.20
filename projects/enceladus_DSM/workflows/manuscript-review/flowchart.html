Workflow design

manuscript-review (Enceladus k_eff manuscript)

Reviews the main text of the Enceladus k_eff manuscript in two rounds, using only the manuscript, the papers it cites and published style authorities: no memory, notes, outline or earlier reviews reach any agent. Round 1 checks content: every citation instance is checked against the cited paper's pages, citation practice is summarized (Molaro et al. 2019 first), terms are compared with the field's usage, and repetition is condensed. Round 2 checks spelling and grammar in American English on the revised text.

Readers (Sonnet) find; checkers (Opus) judge from page images without the readers' reasoning; scripts check quotes, preserved numbers and citations, and compilation. A planted-error calibration gates the run. The user decides every finding at U2 and U3, and the original files are never overwritten.

M1 parse_manuscript.pyscript——Splits the LaTeX into sentences with stable IDs and lists every citation instance (one sentence citing one work).
M2 check_bib.pyscript——Resolves every bibliography entry in Crossref and compares its metadata; finds retractions, missing DOIs, unused and missing keys.
M3 acquire.pyscript——Matches each cited work to a PDF by the DOI or title on its first page.
M4 prepare.pyscript——Renders page images and 300 dpi tiles and runs Marker on every page of every cited paper.
C0 calibrate.py (before the real run)script——Runs every track on a manuscript copy with 40+ planted errors (10+ written by the user) and 10+ controls.
