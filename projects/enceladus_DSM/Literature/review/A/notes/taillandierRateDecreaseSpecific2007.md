- SSA (per mass, CH4 adsorption, accuracy <12%) decay under isothermal (ET; −4, −10, −15 °C; Legagneux data) and TG conditions, lab + Alaska.
- [7]–[8]: isothermal SSA "best fitted using a logarithmic law: SSA(t) = B − A ln(t + Δt)"; it approximates the LSW form SSA0 (τ/(t+τ))^(1/n) over ~0.5–150 days; negative after ~1 yr.
- [26]: "all SSA decay plots can be fitted using the logarithmic equation (1)"; A and B linearly correlated, separate lines for ET and TG.
- ET parameterization (Eqs 10–13): A, B depend on SSA0 and mean temperature Tm; [46] "SSA remains lower at all evolution times when temperature is higher"; rate more T-sensitive with a gradient.
- Density limit: fresh snow <0.18, never >0.28 g/cm3; high-density snow decays slower ([44]).
- [9] Legagneux & Domine 2005: vapour diffusion in air rate-limiting.
