# loweInterfacialStructuralRelaxations2011 — Löwe, Spiegel & Schneebeli 2011, J. Glaciol. 57(203):499–510 (doi 10.3189/002214311796905569)

Whole paper read (pp. 499–510).

**Summary.** X-ray µCT (10 µm voxels, 5.2 mm cubes) of laboratory dendritic snow (initial ρ ≈ 172 kg m^-3, φ_i = 0.188) stored sealed and isothermal at −3.7, −9.5, −18.6 °C for ~1 year. Characterizes coarsening by two-point correlation function, thickness distribution, and inverse SSA; finds no dynamic scaling; SSA length exponent ≈ 1/3 at −3 °C, ≈ 1/4 at −19 °C; anisotropic large-scale rearrangements attributed to gravity. Experimental paper, not a model.

**Findings relevant to the manuscript**
- p.501: "a cube of 520³ voxels = 5.2 mm³ ... which was twice as large as the representative volume of 1–2 mm for the considered properties (Kaempfer and others, 2005)". → source of a "1–2 mm" REV figure is Kaempfer et al. 2005 via Löwe, not Calonne 2011 (whose conductivity REV is 2.5–5.5 mm).
- p.501: z = 3 for diffusion-limited (conserved, LSW) and z = 2 for surface-kinetics-limited transport; "A crossover between these cases can be achieved by a variation of either the diffusion constant of vapor or the kinetic coefficient".
- p.503 Fig. 3: volume fraction increases with time in sealed isothermal samples (densification); "Warmer samples evolve faster than colder samples."
- p.507: at −3 °C "exponent values ... close to 1/z = 0.33 for lssa and lth"; p.508: at −19 °C "lth and lssa follow an evolution which is close to 1/z = 1/4"; attributed tentatively to surface diffusion (z=4), with the caveat that transients may explain it.
- p.508: "The kinetics-limited case requires a small condensation coefficient ... thus the result z = 3 is consistent with diffusion-limited evaporation/condensation."
- p.508: "our snow still has a round appearance, signaling the dominance of relaxations on rough orientations."
- p.501: phase-field (Cahn–Hilliard/Allen–Cahn) framed as "highly idealized starting point" containing LSW physics (cites K&P 2009 for phase-field order parameter).

**Claims it supports / contradicts / qualifies**
- Draft L276 (Löwe 2011 in a list of efforts to "grasp all stages DSM"): supported as an experimental study of isothermal metamorphism; it is not a model and does not treat densification "in the presence of overburden pressures" (sentence before) — fine as a list item.
- Outline L1320 (Löwe as source of SSA decay forms only): **supported** — power-law forms l ~ t^(1/z) with z = 3 (−3 °C), ≈ 4 (−19 °C).
- Outline L852 "L/R̄ = 40, at the 1–2 mm representative volume (Calonne 2011)": Calonne gives 2.5–5.5 mm for conductivity; 1–2 mm is Kaempfer et al. (2005) as quoted by Löwe p.501 — wrong-citation / contradicted for conductivity (keep Calonne's 2.5–5.5 mm).
- Model assumption "porosity constant during isothermal sintering": qualified — sealed isothermal samples densify (Fig. 3), as in Kaempfer & Schneebeli 2007. Limitation sentence: real snow densifies by settling, absent in the model.
- Molaro/Demmenie neck exponents vs SSA exponents: SSA ~ t^(-1/3) (diffusion-limited) or t^(-1/2) (kinetics-limited) are the coarsening-theory analogues — useful to interpret the SSA decay of the k_eff runs at α_c = 10^-3 (kinetics-limited, expect z→2 late) vs α_c = 0.1 (closer to diffusion-limited, z→3). Candidate for Sec. 4/5 if the authors fit SSA(t) exponents.
