# prokschDensitySpecificSurface2015 — Proksch, Löwe & Schneebeli 2015, JGR Earth Surf. 120:346–362 (doi 10.1002/2014JF003266)
Read in full (main text; tables/figs via text layer).
- Method: SnowMicroPen (SMP) penetrometer + statistical model calibrated on micro-CT; density/corr. length/SSA errors 10.6/16.4/23.1% (abstract). SSA from slope of correlation function at origin, per ice volume (Sect. 2.1.1).
- p.1 Intro: "With density and SSA ... key properties as thermal conductivity, dielectric permittivity, or air permeability can be computed [Arons and Colbeck, 1995; Mätzler, 1996; Calonne et al., 2012]" — but "strictly not sufficient to characterize snow microstructure [Löwe 2013]".
- Sect 5.1: other SSA methods 10–15% error (NIR photography 15% Matzl; DUFISSS <12% Gallet; IRIS 10%).
Claims:
- Outline L1318–1320 "SSA measured by NIR reflectance, gas adsorption and tomography (Gallet, Matzl, Proksch)": PARTIAL — Proksch is penetrometry (SMP) calibrated by micro-CT; cite it for penetrometry/tomography, not as a separate method category without saying so. For a lab test on small ice-sphere samples, micro-CT is the relevant one.
- Supports framing that SSA (with density) is used to compute conductivity (prior idea; cite with Calonne 2011/2019, Löwe 2013 not in lib).
