# libbrechtMeasurementsSurfaceAttachment2013 — Libbrecht & Rickerby 2013, J. Cryst. Growth 377:1–8 (doi 10.1016/j.jcrysgro.2013.04.037)

(Correct PDF, replaced per Addendum. Temperature signs read from context: "−40 < T < −2 °C"; −12, −8.3, −5.15, −3 °C sample data.)

**Summary.** Growth-rate measurements of the basal and prism facets of small (< 50 μm) simple-prism ice crystals resting on a sapphire substrate, at reduced pressure (p < 0.025 bar) with a nearby ice reservoir setting σ, −40 to −2 °C, > 200 crystals; only facets parallel to the substrate used; residual diffusion corrected (Eq. 2). Attachment coefficients fit a polynucleation model α = A exp(−σ0/σ_surf); A = 1 except prism above −10 °C.

**Findings relevant to the manuscript**
- Definition p.1: "v(T, σ_surf) = α(T, σ_surf) v_kin(T) σ_surf ... From the definition of v_kin, we must have α ≤ 1."
- Layer nucleation p.1–2: "observing a functional form α(σ_surf) ∼ exp(−σ0/σ_surf) ... is strong evidence that the growth is limited mainly by the nucleation of molecular layers"; conclusion (1) p.6: "well described by a polynucleation model with the simplified parameterization α(T, σ_surf) = A exp(−σ0/σ_surf)".
- σ0 values: basal −12 °C ⟨σ0⟩ = 1.95 ± 0.15%; −5.15 °C 0.70 ± 0.10%; prism −8.3 °C 1.4 ± 0.3%; prism −3 °C A = 0.085, σ0 = 0.03% (Figs. 5, 7).
- Diffusion vs kinetics p.2, Eq. 1: σ_surf = α_diff/(α + α_diff) σ∞ with α_diff ≈ 0.15/(p R_μm) — at 1 atm diffusion limits growth when α ≫ 0.15/R_μm; "it can be exceedingly difficult to use growth measurements to extract useful information about α".
- Prior experiments "affected" by diffusion and substrate systematics (p.2–3).
- γ ≈ 0.11 J m^-2 (p.6).
- No statement of a "range 10^-3–10^-1" in this paper (whole paper read).
