# flinThreedimensionalGeometricMeasurements2004 — Flin, Brzoska, Lesaffre, Coléou & Pieritz 2004, Ann. Glaciol. 38:39–44 (doi 10.3189/172756404781814942)

**Summary.** Image-analysis tools (porosity, SSA by voxel projection, normal-vector anisotropy, adaptive mean curvature) applied to μCT images (4.91 μm, analysed at 9.82 μm) from a 3-month isothermal metamorphism experiment at −2 °C on natural new snow (Col de Porte, 2002), stored in a closed box; 2.5 mm analysis cubes.

**Findings relevant to the manuscript**
- Porosity decreased with time following "the classical dry-snow packing laws" (p.4, Fig. 2) — isothermal snow settles/densifies.
- SSA logarithmic in time p.4 (42): "SSA(t) = −157.23 log(t) + 681.52" (correlation coefficient 0.986), "in fairly good agreement with the results based on CH4 adsorption (Cabanes 2003; Legagneux 2003)". (Sign lost in the text layer; SSA decreases.)
- Slight persistent vertical anisotropy after 66 h attributed to gravity/packing (p.4–5).
- Curvature distribution sharpens and mean curvature decreases (rounding, coarsening) (p.5).
- Isothermal metamorphism near 0 °C: "a reduced influence of possible surface or bulk diffusion at grain boundaries" expected (p.1).
