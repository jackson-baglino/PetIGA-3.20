# calonneStudyTemperatureGradient2014 — Calonne, Flin, Geindreau, Lesaffre & Rolland du Roscoat 2014, The Cryosphere 8:2255–2274 (doi 10.5194/tc-8-2255-2014)

**Summary.** Cold-room TG experiment: sieved rounded grains, 300 ± 15 kg m^-3 (slab), first 24 h isothermal at −5 °C to re-sinter, then 3 weeks at 43 K m^-1 (plates −7/−1 °C, room −4 °C). Seven impregnated samples (0–500 h) imaged by μCT (8.4–9.7 μm). Computed density, directional SSA, correlation lengths, curvatures, tortuosities, k tensor (GeoDict homogenization, k_ice 2.107, k_air 0.024), permeability; k and K readjusted to 294 kg m^-3 via Calonne 2011/2012 regressions (Eqs. 23–24); compared with self-consistent ellipsoid estimates.

**Findings relevant to the manuscript** (Table 1 checked on the rendered PDF page 4)
- Density constant: p.9 (2263) "The snow density shows no significant evolution with time and the average value over the experiment is 294 kg m^−3"; sample densities 275–315 (Table 1).
- SSA p.9: "decreases continuously with time from 27.7 to 13.4 m² kg^−1".
- k two-stage p.12 (2266): "Computed and readjusted values decrease from 0 to 73 h and then continuously increase until the end"; mechanism p.16 (2270) "(i) the destruction of connections between grains during the first period ... limits the heat conduction ... (ii) the growth of large ice structures ... enhances the heat transfer." Agrees with Schneebeli & Sokratov (2004) two-stage evolution (−8 °C, 100 K m^-1, 268 kg m^-3).
- Table 1 k (W m^-1 K^-1): 0A (0 h, 315 kg m^-3) x/y/z 0.22/0.23/0.21; 1A (73 h, 275) 0.14/0.14/0.15; 7G (500 h, 310) 0.20/0.19/0.26. My recomputation with Eq. 23 (k_fit(294)=0.2039): k_z 0.18 → 0.17 → 0.23; k_xy 0.20 → 0.16 → 0.18; orientation average (k_z+2k_xy)/3 0.193 → 0.196 (both ≈ 0.19).
- Anisotropy p.12: "A(k) increases from 0.90 to 1.34", largest increase 0–144 h; A(τ_i) "increases from 0.86 to 1.90 between 0 and 144 h and then slightly decreases to reach 1.70 at the end"; A(l_c) 0.96 → 1.25; A(K) 0.91 → 1.23; A(l_SSA) 0.92 → 1.01.
- Self-consistent estimates p.13–15: E(k_z) = −22.6 ± 4.3%, E(k_xy) = −15.6 ± 6.0%, E(A(k)) = −7.9 ± 7.4%; "without any fitting parameters".
- Conclusion p.17 (2271): "confirm that the density alone, or any isotropic quantity, is not sufficient to describe the time evolution of physical properties during a TG metamorphism."
- p.16 (2270): "Figure 6 shows an overall increase of the effective thermal conductivity and permeability at a constant density, underlying the role of the microstructure rearrangement (Schneebeli and Sokratov, 2004; Satyawali et al., 2008)."
- Intro p.2: "Schneebeli and Sokratov (2004) and Satyawali et al. (2008) have shown that during TG metamorphism, the effective thermal conductivity of snow evolves without significant changes in density".
- The paper never relates k to SSA (whole paper read).
- Off-diagonal tensor terms negligible (p.7).

**Claims it supports / contradicts / qualifies**
- Outline L510–513 (gap: Schneebeli & Sokratov 2004 and Calonne 2014 followed conductivity at constant density under a gradient): **supported** (p.2, p.16).
- Outline L525–550 (\Note on Calonne 2014): 294 kg m^-3, 43 K m^-1, −4 °C, 3 weeks, seven samples 0–500 h, SSA 27.7 → 13.4, densities 275–315, Eq. 23, two stages with fall over 0–73 h, A(k) 0.90 → 1.34 mostly in first 144 h, l_c 0.96 → 1.25, l_SSA 0.92 → 1.01, never relates k to SSA, self-consistent model low by 23/16/8%, "density alone, or any isotropic quantity, is not sufficient": **all supported**. Corrections: (a) ice tortuosity anisotropy is 0.86 → 1.90 at 144 h, then 1.70 at the end (outline gives only 0.86 → 1.90); (b) reconstructed k_xy at 73 h is 0.16 (outline 0.15; within its ±0.01); (c) the outline's "orientation average ends where it started" is my recomputation too, but Calonne et al. themselves describe "an overall increase of the effective thermal conductivity ... at a constant density" (p.16) — the manuscript should say the average is our reconstruction and that Calonne's increase is mainly in k_z. Also "under 43 K m^-1 at −4 °C": −4 °C is the room; the slab spans −7 to −1 °C (mean ≈ −4 °C) — fine.
- Outline L1276–1280 (Discussion 5.3, "under a temperature gradient conductivity changes at constant density, with SSA and anisotropy"): supported.
- Outline L557–560 ("under a gradient, losing surface area did not raise conductivity, because the surface was lost by coarsening and bond sublimation"): supported in the sense of the reconstructed average; Calonne attribute the first-stage drop to "destruction of connections between grains" (p.16).
