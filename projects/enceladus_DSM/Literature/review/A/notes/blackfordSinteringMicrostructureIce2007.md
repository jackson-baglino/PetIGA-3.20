# blackfordSinteringMicrostructureIce2007 — Blackford 2007, J. Phys. D 40:R355–R385 (doi 10.1088/0022-3727/40/21/R02)

**Summary.** Topical review (PDF p.1 is a download cover; journal pages R355–R385 = PDF p.2–32). Covers sintering basics (driving force, transport mechanisms, stages, maps), experimental techniques (light microscopy, LTSEM, XMT, gas adsorption), pressureless sintering of ice spheres (Kingery 1960, Kuroiwa, Hobbs & Mason 1964, Maeno & Ebinuma 1983), pressure sintering of firn, non-isothermal metamorphism (briefly), recent models (Colbeck 1998/2001, Miller 2003, Legagneux & Dominé 2005, Flin 2003/2004), liquid-phase sintering, mechanical consequences.

**Findings relevant to the manuscript**
- Abstract p.2 (R355): "The main mechanism for isothermal sintering of ice particles is by vapour diffusion; however other transport mechanisms certainly contribute."
- Surface transport does not densify: p.4 (R357) "Bulk transport mechanisms lead to shrinkage and the mutual approach of the centres of two particles whereas surface transport mechanisms do not lead to shrinkage." Also p.28 (R381) "Vapour diffusion does not lead to densification." → supports outline "Scope" bullet (vapor-transport sintering does not densify; porosity constant).
- **Kuczynski power law and exponents** p.14 (R367): "(X/D)^n = Bt/D^m ... surface diffusion, n = 7, m = 4; lattice diffusion, n = 5, m = 3; vapour transport, n = 3, m = 2; viscous or plastic flow, n = 2, m = 1."
- **Hobbs & Mason's vapour model gives n = 5, not 3**: p.16 (R369) "Hobbs and Mason's modified model gave values of n = 5 and m = 3 for vapour transport; these are the same values as Kucynzski determined for volume diffusion." Reason: Kuczynski's vapour model assumed mean free path large vs neck; H&M used diffusion through air (p.16: "This is not valid as MFP = 0.1 μm and neck radius >1 μm").
- H&M experiments: 50–700 μm diameter, −3 to −20 °C, vapour dominant; measurements "a factor of five lower than their theoretical prediction for vapour transport" (p.16 R369). In unsaturated air neck growth stopped after ~0.3 h and grains then sublimated (p.15–16, R368–369).
- Kingery 1960 concluded surface diffusion; rejected by H&M as implausible (p.14 R367: "an activation energy of 1.2 eV ... so it cannot be correct").
- Maeno & Ebinuma 1983 sintering map (35 μm spheres): vapour dominant under most conditions; surface diffusion only for neck radii < ~2 μm at 0 to −6 °C and < ~1 μm below ~−40 °C (p.17 R370).
- Gas adsorption measures SSA; "for surface transport (although there is no densification the structure coarsens and there is a decrease in SSA)" (p.14 R367).
- Legagneux & Dominé: SSA = b − a ln(t + Δt), "the equation has no physical basis" (p.22 R375).
- Snow densification "cannot occur by vapour diffusion" (p.18 R371).
- Dihedral angle 145° (γ_sv = 109 mJ m^-2, γ_ss = 65 mJ m^-2; Ketcham & Hobbs 1969) p.7 (R360) — the γ = 0.109 J m^-2 in Table 1 matches this value.
- Grain-boundary-groove neck geometry is the correct one; concave-neck models (incl. H&M) are "incorrect" (p.28 R381).
