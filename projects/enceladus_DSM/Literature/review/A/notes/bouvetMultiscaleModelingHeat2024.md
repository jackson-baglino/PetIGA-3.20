# bouvetMultiscaleModelingHeat2024 — Bouvet, Calonne, Flin & Geindreau 2024, The Cryosphere 18:4285–4313 (doi 10.5194/tc-18-4285-2024) — UNCITED, closely matching
Read (theory, transition analysis, conclusions; experimental comparisons scanned).
- Homogenization of multiple-scale expansions over periodic REVs (ε = l/L ≪ 1) for heat + vapour in dry snow with α from 1e-10 to 1; cell problems with periodic vectors.
- Abstract: "A transition α value arises, of the order of 10−4, for typical snow microstructures (∼0.5 mm), such that the vapor transport is limited by sublimation–deposition below that value and by diffusion above it."
- αT "range[s] between 10−3 for small grains (∼0.1 mm) and 10−5 for very large grains (∼5 mm)"; estimated 3e-4 for their case.
- Below αT (model A), k_eff = conductive cell-problem conductivity; above, an apparent conductivity enhanced by sublimation–deposition (models C/D).
- Indirect pore-scale estimates of α: 1e-4 to 1e-3.
Claims / additions:
- k_eff here is the conductive (model-A-type) tensor at α_c = 1e-3 with ~0.17 mm grains, i.e. near αT ≈ 1e-3 for small grains: the purely conductive k_eff is the right steady quantity in the sublimation-limited regime and a lower bound above it (with Fourteau 2021).
