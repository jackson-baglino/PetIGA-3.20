# grangerOrientationSelectiveGrain2021 — Granger, Flin, Ludwig, Hammad & Geindreau 2021, The Cryosphere 15:4381–4398 (doi 10.5194/tc-15-4381-2021)

**Summary.** In situ diffraction contrast tomography (DCT, 6.5 μm) of a 4 mm rounded-grain snow sample (476 kg m^-3) under a downward TG (17 then 52 °C m^-1) at mean −2.1 to −4.1 °C for 3 d, in vacuum (CellDyM2); ~900 grain orientations tracked; local interface fluxes vs. c-axis orientation.

**Findings relevant to the manuscript**
- Facets form under TG (p.6): "a strong faceting of the downward-oriented surfaces".
- Orientation dependence of kinetics p.1: "the kinetic coefficient is not equal on the prismatic and basal faces of ice"; result: "higher sublimation–deposition rates for grains with their c axis in the horizontal plane at the beginning" (abstract).
- High-index (rough, α ≈ 45°) surfaces "have higher deposition coefficient and are more active" (p.14).
- Density constant (476 ± 5%), SSA 18 → ~20 m² kg^-1 (p.7–8); REV for density and SSA ≈ 2.34 mm (p.6–8).
- Under TG > 10–20 °C m^-1 interfaces can be "significantly out of equilibrium" (p.1).

**Claims it supports / contradicts / qualifies**
- Outline L1220 (cite Granger 2021 "for faceting under a gradient" in the α_c paragraph): **supported** — faceting under TG and orientation-dependent attachment kinetics (p.1, p.6, p.14). Useful as evidence that a single constant α is an effective average over surface orientations (pairs with Braun 2024).
- Note: Granger's conditions are TG, not isothermal; it supports only "α varies with surface orientation/faceting", not anything about isothermal sintering.
