# wangComparisonEffectsUnidirectional2017 — Wang & Baker, Hydrol. Process. 31, 871–879 (2017)
Read in full (short paper, 9 pp).
## Summary
1D arrays of polycrystalline ice spheres in a plastic tube under unidirectional and sign-alternating temperature gradients, µCT; depth-hoar-like protrusions form at necks following vapor-transfer direction; alternating gradients give symmetric protrusions bridging spheres.
## Findings
- "polycrystalline ice spheres have higher sintering rates, which are attributed to larger areas of high-index faces (Hobbs & Mason, 1964)" (p.1–2).
- Isothermal metamorphism "often interpreted as a sintering process (Colbeck, 1998)" (p.1).
