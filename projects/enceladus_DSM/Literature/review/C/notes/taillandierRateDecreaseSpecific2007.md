# taillandierRateDecreaseSpecific2007 — Taillandier, Domine, Simpson, Sturm & Douglas, JGR 112, F03003 (2007)
DOI (PDF p.1): 10.1029/2006JF000514. Read in full (13 pp). Minus signs are lost in the text layer: isothermal runs were at −4, −10, −15 °C (cold room), TG runs at −15 °C with base at −5 °C (33 K/m).

## Summary
SSA (CH4 adsorption at 77 K, 6% reproducibility, 12% accuracy) of fresh snow monitored for months under isothermal (−4, −10, −15 °C) and temperature-gradient (33 K/m) laboratory conditions, and in the Alaskan taiga snowpack (natural TG and quasi-isothermal "tables"). All decays fit SSA = B − A ln(t + Δt); A and B linearly correlated with distinct lines for equitemperature (ET) and TG regimes; empirical rate equations in SSA0 and mean temperature.

## Findings relevant to the manuscript
- Logarithmic decay in time: "In all cases, the SSA decay follows a logarithmic equation with three adjustable variables" (abstract, p.1); logarithmic fit best (R² 0.94 vs exponential 0.72, power 0.90, linear 0.51) (p.6). Eq. 1 is an approximation of the LSW form SSA0(τ/(t+τ))^{1/n} valid ~0.5–150 days (p.2; p.10).
- Temperature accelerates the decay: "Higher temperatures accelerate the decrease in SSA, and this decrease is faster under TG than ET conditions" (abstract, p.1); temperature dependence "expected, as sublimation and condensation rates increase with the water vapor pressure" (p.7).
- ET regime driven by curvature: "curvature-driven metamorphism in the ET case ... and temperature gradient–driven metamorphism in the other case"; threshold between 9 and 20 °C/m (p.10).
- Density limitation: equations "may not apply for snow densities greater than 0.30 g cm⁻³"; dense windpack decays much slower (p.10–11). Our packings (ice fraction 0.525–0.725, i.e., ~480–665 kg/m³) are far denser.
- Mean-field model of Legagneux & Domine 2005 found "diffusion of water vapor in air was the rate-limiting step" (p.2) — relevant to the Λ discussion (attachment vs diffusion), but contrasts with our α_c = 1e-3 regime.

## Claims supported / contradicted / qualified
- Outline L1050 and L1317 (SSA against log time in isothermal snow; Legagneux & Domine 2005; Taillandier 2007; "functional form only"): supported as functional form (p.1, p.6). Caveat: fresh, low-density natural snow (<0.3 g/cm³), with compaction; our result is on dense 2D packings.
- Section 5.2 (temperature only rescales time): Taillandier's ET parameterization (Eq. 13) depends on SSA0 and Tm in a way that is not a pure time shift; their data at −4/−10/−15 °C share one A–B line "regardless of the temperature" (p.7) — loosely consistent with a single curve family, but they do not test time–temperature superposition. Do not cite as support for superposition.
