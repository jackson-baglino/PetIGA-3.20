# jaumannDistributionIcyParticles2008 — Jaumann et al., Icarus 193, 407–419 (2008)
Read (13 pp; spectral-model details skimmed).

## Summary
Cassini VIMS water-ice band depths mapped to particle diameters across Enceladus.

## Findings
- "particle diameter of water ice increases toward younger tectonically altered surface units ... The largest particles (∼0.2 mm) are concentrated in the so called 'tiger stripes'" (abstract).
- ~90% of area (cratered, fractured plains) dominated by particles < 30 µm; tectonically deformed regions 30–100 µm; tiger stripe distribution shifted ×2 larger (p. ~9).
- Interpretation: eruption dynamics plus surface weathering (sputtering, E-ring impacts, impact gardening cause comminution); large particles attributed to cryovolcanic origin / freshness, not sintering (p. ~10).

## Claims
- Outline L1329–1331 (grain size increasing toward the fractures in VIMS data): supported (abstract). Do not present it as evidence of sintering: Jaumann attribute it to fresh material and eruption dynamics; sintering/annealing as cause is Spencer 2009/Kempf 2010's hypothesis.
- Grain sizes in VIMS are optical (tens of µm–0.2 mm), not plume-grain sizes.
