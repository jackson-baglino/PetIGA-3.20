# hortonSpectralMeasurementsSurface2017 — Horton & Jamieson, J. Glaciol. 63(239), 477–486 (2017)
Read (10 pp).
## Summary
Field spectrometer reflectance (750–2500 nm) of 377 snow samples incl. 161 surface hoar; critical weak-layer types (surface hoar, near-surface facets, depth hoar) less reflective in IR.
## Findings
- Buried plate-shaped surface hoar layers "are prone to releasing dangerous snow-slab avalanches" (abstract).
