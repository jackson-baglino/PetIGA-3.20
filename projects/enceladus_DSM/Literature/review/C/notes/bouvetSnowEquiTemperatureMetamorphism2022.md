# bouvetSnowEquiTemperatureMetamorphism2022 — Bouvet, Calonne, Flin & Geindreau, JAMES 14, e2022MS002998 (2022)
DOI (PDF): 10.1029/2022MS002998. Read (HAL version, ~30 pp; SI not in library).

## Summary
Volume-conserving mean-curvature-flow phase-field model (Bretin et al. 2019; kinetics-limited, no vapor diffusion) applied to µCT images of snow for equi-temperature metamorphism (ETM). Time calibrated through α by fitting SSA of Flin et al. (2004) at −2 °C: α = (9.8 ± 0.7)×10⁻⁴; evaluated against Hagenmuller et al. (2019). Then keff, vapor diffusivity and permeability tensors computed by periodic homogenization (Auriault et al. 2009; GeoDict) on simulated images of four samples over 70–80 days.

## Findings
- "the condensation coefficient α ... Relating the numerical time ... to the real physical time involves the condensation coefficient" (abstract) — α only sets the time scale in a kinetics-limited curvature-flow model.
- α = (9.8 ± 0.7)×10⁻⁴ at −2 °C, "in the lower range of the values in the literature" (p. ~25).
- **keff during isothermal metamorphism at nearly constant density**: I17 0.078 → 0.081 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹; Grad3 (depth hoar) 0.37 → 0.42 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹; density drift only 1–3% (numerical artefact) (Sect. 3.2.2, Figs. 8–9). Anisotropy enhanced for initially anisotropic microstructures (abstract).
- "simulations show an enhancement of the structural anisotropy" and "the influence of microstructure overcomes the one of density" for vapor diffusion (p. ~24).

## Claims
- Draft L501 rescaling time through α: the procedure is in this paper (2022), not Bouvet 2023 — fix citation (outline L1203–1205 already says so).
- Draft L276 "Bouvet 2022 used a phase-field model to predict microstructural evolution for dry snow under equitemperature conditions": supported; add "and computed the resulting conductivity".
- **Draft L279 "How metamorphism influences the macroscopic properties in the absence of compaction is still unquantified" and outline L508–512 "What is not quantified is the isothermal case: sintering alone, at fixed porosity": contradicted.** Bouvet et al. (2022) computed keff (and permeability, vapor diffusivity) during isothermal metamorphism of snow at essentially constant density, finding small increases (+4% and +14%). The gap must be narrowed: granular packings sintering from contacts (neck growth), the conductivity–SSA relation, temperature and porosity dependence, vapor-diffusion–limited kinetics in air.
- Outline L1215 (α = (9.8 ± 0.7)×10⁻⁴ at −2 °C): supported.
