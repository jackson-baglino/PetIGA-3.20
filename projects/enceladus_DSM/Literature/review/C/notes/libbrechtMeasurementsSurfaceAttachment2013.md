# libbrechtMeasurementsSurfaceAttachment2013 — Libbrecht & Rickerby, J. Cryst. Growth 377, 1–8 (2013)
DOI (PDF p.1): 10.1016/j.jcrysgro.2013.04.037. Read in full (8 pp; replaced correct PDF).

## Summary
Growth rates of isolated faceted ice prisms (<50 µm) on sapphire at p < 0.025 atm, −40 to about −2 °C (data at −12 and −5.15 °C shown), basal and prism facets, >200 crystals; attachment coefficient α(T, σsurf) inferred from v = α vkin σsurf after diffusion corrections.

## Findings
- "All our growth measurements were well described using the functional form α(σsurf) = A exp(−σ0/σsurf), consistent with a polynucleation model" (p.4); A = 1 for basal and for prism at T ≤ −10 °C, A < 1 for prism above −10 °C (p.4–6).
- Basal σ0: ⟨σ0⟩ = 1.95 ± 0.15% at −12 °C; 0.70 ± 0.10% at −5.15 °C (Fig. 5 caption, p.5).
- In air (p ≈ 1) growth is diffusion-limited: αdiff ≈ 0.15/(p R_µm) (p.2); "diffusion corrections are considerably more difficult to calculate than was previously appreciated" (p.3); claims that α depends on air pressure are "questionable" (p.2–3).
- Surface energy "γ ≈ 0.11 J/m² of the ice/vapor interface", step height 0.32 nm (p.5).
- Quasi-liquid layer and facet-dependent kinetics (p.5–6).

## Claims
- Draft L273 "Libbrecht (2017) led an experiment in near vacuum to quantify how the critical supersaturation varies with temperature ... for crystal growth on the basal plane": wrong-citation — the experiment is Libbrecht & Rickerby (2013), and it measured basal and prism facets (p.1, p.4–5). Libbrecht 2017 is a review.
- Draft L273 "α ∈ [10⁻³, 10⁻¹]" (no citation): not in this paper; α here is a function of σsurf (→1 at σsurf ≫ σ0; exponentially small for σsurf ≪ σ0). For σsurf ~10⁻⁴ (the neck driving force, outline L1140–1143) a faceted surface would have α = exp(−σ0/σsurf) ≈ 0; a constant α_c is a rough-surface idealization. State this as a limitation (not a new claim about results).
- Table 1 γ = 0.109 J m⁻²: consistent (≈0.11, p.5).

## Candidate additions
- Intro para 5: replace Libbrecht 2017 with Libbrecht & Rickerby 2013 for the measurement; give the form α = A exp(−σ0/σsurf).
- Limitations: constant isotropic α_c vs. nucleation-limited facets (p.4–5).
