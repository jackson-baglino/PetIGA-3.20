# grangerOrientationSelectiveGrain2021 — Granger, Flin, Ludwig, Hammad & Geindreau, The Cryosphere 15, 4381–4398 (2021)
DOI (PDF p.1): 10.5194/tc-15-4381-2021. Read in full (18 pp).

## Summary
In situ time-lapse diffraction contrast tomography (DCT) plus absorption µCT of a rounded-grain snow sample (476 kg/m³, 4 mm cylinder) under 17 then 52 °C/m at mean −2.1 to −4.1 °C for 3 days in vacuum (CellDyM2). Orientations of ~900 grains followed; local interface fluxes binned by crystallographic and geometric angles.

## Findings relevant to the manuscript
- Attachment kinetics depend on crystal orientation: "the kinetic coefficient is not equal on the prismatic and basal faces of ice" (p.1); "surfaces with α = 45°, which have high index orientations, are rougher at the molecular level, they have higher deposition coefficient and are more active" (p.14/4394); prismatic faces more active than basal at T ≥ −4 °C (p.14).
- Higher sublimation–deposition rates for grains with c axis horizontal early on (abstract, p.1).
- TG metamorphism at constant density: "grain size increases while the density remains constant" (p.1); density constant at 476 ± 5% (p.7).
- SSA rose slightly from 18 to ~20 m²/kg under TG (p.8) — faceting can raise SSA.
- REV for density and SSA ~2–2.34 mm (p.6–8).

## Claims supported / contradicted / qualified
- Outline L1220 (cite box for Section 5.1 "α_c as an effective parameter": "Granger 2021 for faceting under a gradient"): supported in the sense that attachment differs between facets and rough high-index surfaces (p.14), so any single α_c is a surface average. This is the relevant point; their conditions (TG, vacuum) differ from ours. Closely matching only for "α_c varies with surface orientation".
- Outline L852–853 (domain 2 mm, RVE 1–2 mm): Granger's REV for density and SSA is ~2–2.34 mm (p.6–8) in 3D snow — consistent order.
