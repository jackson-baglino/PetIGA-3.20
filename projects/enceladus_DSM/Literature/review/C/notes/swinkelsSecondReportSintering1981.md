# swinkelsSecondReportSintering1981 — Swinkels & Ashby, Acta Metall. 29, 259–281 (1981)
Read in full (23 pp, OCR scan; Table 2 equations garbled, taken via Molaro's transcription).

## Summary
"Second report" on sintering-mechanism diagrams (first report: Ashby 1974). Six mechanisms (surface diffusion, lattice diffusion from surface, vapour transport, boundary diffusion, lattice diffusion from boundary and from dislocations); only 4–6 densify (Fig. 1). New: coupling of boundary diffusion with surface redistribution, Stage 1 → Stage 3 interpolation (Stage 2 omitted for spheres), particle rearrangement. Diagrams for wires and spheres of Ag, Cu, Ni, Fe, W, NaCl, stainless steel; aims for rates within a factor of 2.

## Findings
- "Six or more distinguishable mechanisms contribute to neck growth and densification" (p.1); "Only mechanisms 4, 5 and 6 cause densification" (Fig. 1 caption).
- Vapor transport dominant only in one data set (NaCl; agrees with Kingery & Coble); metals mostly surface/lattice/boundary diffusion (p. ~17–20).
- Non-densifying mechanisms multiplied by smoothing factor S (Eq. 3.2), e.g. vapour transport driven by (K3 − Km)·S.
- No ice application.

## Claims
- Draft L262 "a sintering model originally used in metallurgy first proposed by Swinkels and Ashby (1981)": partially — SA81 is the second report; the diagram method was first proposed by Ashby (1974) (p.1 "described in an earlier paper [1]"). Say "developed by".
- Outline Appendix A (equations via Molaro Table 3; smoothing S): consistent with SA81 structure (S factor, curvature K3 − Km) — supported at the level checkable from the OCR.
