# prokschDensitySpecificSurface2015 — Proksch, Löwe & Schneebeli, JGR Earth Surf. 120, 346–362 (2015)
Read (abstract, methods, results, conclusions; statistical-model derivation skimmed).

## Summary
Statistical model retrieving density, correlation length and SSA from SnowMicroPen force profiles, calibrated with µCT (Alpine, Arctic, Antarctic snow); errors 10.6%, 16.4%, 23.1%; 2D stratigraphy at Kohnen Station.

## Claims
- Outline L562 / L1318–1320 (SSA measurable in the field): supported — SSA from SMP in < 1 min per metre profile (abstract), plus µCT.
- Supports measuring correlation length (relevant if the paper relates k to correlation length like Calonne 2014/2019).
