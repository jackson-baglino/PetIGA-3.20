# abramovEndogenicHeatEnceladus2009 — Abramov & Spencer, Icarus 199, 189–196 (2009)
Read in full (8 pp).
## Summary
Thermal model of conductive surface heating by warm tiger-stripe fractures/vents, fitted to CIRS spectra (July 2005, Nov 2006).
## Findings
- Best fit: narrow vents up to 223 K; 130–155 K vents need widths 180/22 m and cannot supply the vapor flux (abstract).
- "Models with a 10 cm thick upper insulating layer produce a poor match ... suggesting high thermal inertias near the tiger stripes" (abstract; p.5).
## Claims / additions
- Not cited. Closely relevant to outline Section 6 (conductivity near the Tiger Stripes): an observational hint of high thermal inertia (not an insulating fluffy layer) near the stripes. Could be cited as such, without attributing it to sintering (they do not).
