Read (main text; Knudsen-regime details skimmed).
## Summary
Conduction model for mono-sized spheres under stagnant gas (kinetic theory, Knudsen regimes), with radiative and solid-contact terms; reproduces grain-size and pressure dependence and a high-pressure plateau.
