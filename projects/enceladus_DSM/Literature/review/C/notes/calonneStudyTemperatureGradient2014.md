# calonneStudyTemperatureGradient2014 — Calonne, Flin, Geindreau, Lesaffre & Rolland du Roscoat, The Cryosphere 8, 2255–2274 (2014)
DOI (PDF p.1): 10.5194/tc-8-2255-2014. Read in full (20 pp). Table 1 checked on rendered PDF p.4 (printed 2258); readjusted conductivities recomputed from Table 1 and Eq. 23.

## Summary
Sieved rounded-grain snow slab (100 × 50 × 14 cm, ~300 kg/m³), 24 h isothermal at −5 °C, then 3 weeks at mean −4 °C under 43 K/m (plates at −7 and −1 °C). Seven samples (0–500 h) impregnated and imaged by µCT (8.4–9.7 µm). Computed: density, directional SSA, correlation lengths, curvatures, air/ice tortuosity, conductivity tensor (homogenization, Auriault et al. 2009; k_ice = 2.107, k_air = 0.024 W/m/K), permeability; anisotropy coefficients; comparison with self-consistent ellipsoid estimates.

## Findings relevant to the manuscript (verified)
- Density constant (mean 294 kg/m³; samples 275–315 due to initial heterogeneity) (p.9/2263; Table 1).
- SSA (x+y+z)/3: 27.7 → 23.4 → 20.8 → 18.2 → 15.0 → 14.9 → 13.4 m²/kg at 0, 73, 144, 217, 313, 409, 500 h (Table 1).
- Raw k (W/m/K), Table 1: kx 0.22, 0.14, 0.14, 0.14, 0.19, 0.16, 0.20; ky 0.23, 0.14, 0.14, 0.14, 0.18, 0.17, 0.19; kz 0.21, 0.15, 0.18, 0.18, 0.25, 0.21, 0.26.
- Density readjustment Eq. 23, k_r = k·k_fit(294)/k_fit(ρs), k_fit = 2.5e-6 ρ² − 1.23e-4 ρ + 0.024 (p.7/2261). My recomputation: kz_r 0.184, 0.171, 0.194, 0.205, 0.219, 0.222, 0.234; kxy_r 0.197, 0.159, 0.151, 0.159, 0.162, 0.174, 0.176; mean 0.192 → 0.163 (73 h) → 0.195 (500 h).
- Two stages: "Computed and readjusted values decrease from 0 to 73 h and then continuously increase until the end ... After 73 h, the z values become much higher" (p.12/2266). Explanation: "(i) the destruction of connections between grains during the first period ... limits the heat conduction ... (ii) the growth of large ice structures in the second period ... enhances the heat transfer" (p.16/2270).
- Anisotropy coefficients (Fig. 7, p.12/2266): A(k) 0.90 → 1.34; A(τi) 0.86 → 1.90 at 144 h then 1.70 at end; A(lc) 0.96 → 1.25; A(K) 0.91 → 1.23; A(τa) 0.97 → 1.13; A(lSSA) 0.92 → 1.01.
- Self-consistent estimate errors: E(kz) = −22.6 ± 4.3%, E(kxy) = −15.6 ± 6.0%, E(A(k)) = −7.9 ± 7.4% (p.14–15).
- Conclusion: "the density alone, or any isotropic quantity, is not sufficient to describe the time evolution of physical properties during a TG metamorphism" (p.17/2271).
- Prior art on conductivity changing at constant density: "Schneebeli and Sokratov (2004) and Satyawali et al. (2008) have shown that during TG metamorphism, the effective thermal conductivity of snow evolves without significant changes in density, but only because of the ice/pore reorganization" (p.2/2256). Schneebeli & Sokratov conditions per Calonne: mean −8 °C, 100 K/m, 268 kg/m³ (p.16/2270).
- "Heat conduction in snow is mostly due to the conduction of ice ... strongly linked to its density and to the ice tortuosity" (p.16/2270).
- SSA is never related to k in this paper.
