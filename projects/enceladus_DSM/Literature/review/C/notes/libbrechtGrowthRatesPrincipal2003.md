# libbrechtGrowthRatesPrincipal2003 — Libbrecht, J. Cryst. Growth 247, 530–540 (2003)
Read (11 pp).
## Summary
Growth rates of basal and prism facets from vapor at −39 to −10 °C (text layer garbles signs); data described by 2D-nucleation-limited growth; critical supersaturations similar for both facets, decreasing with T.
