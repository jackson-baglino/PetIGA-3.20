## Summary
Rotating-drum flowability of ice powders (analogues of fresh plume deposits) at ~90 K upwards in a sealed cryostat (no sintering at the coldest temperatures); cohesion increases with temperature; lower gravity on Enceladus gives very loose, highly porous regolith.
