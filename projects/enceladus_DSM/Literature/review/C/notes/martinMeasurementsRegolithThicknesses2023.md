# martinMeasurementsRegolithThicknesses2023 — Martin et al., Icarus 392, 115369 (2023)
Read (main text; mapping tables skimmed).
## Summary
Enceladus regolith thicknesses from tectonic pit chains (Slope Angle Method): up to 700 m, mean 250 m (n = 116); combined with plume deposition-rate models (jet vs curtain) to bound accumulation time; deposition rates depend on unknown regolith density/porosity.
## Findings
- "we measured regolith thicknesses up to 700 m (mean = 250 m, n = 116)" (abstract).
- "Modification of regolith post-deposition might include annealing or sintering regolith particles (e.g. Molaro et al., 2019) reducing porosity and increasing cohesion ... over geologic timescales" (Sect. 5).
