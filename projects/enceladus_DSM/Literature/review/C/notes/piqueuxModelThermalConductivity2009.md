# piqueuxModelThermalConductivity2009 — Piqueux & Christensen, JGR 114, E09006 (2009), Part 2: cemented soils
Read (12 pp; model details skimmed).
## Summary
Numerical conduction model for spherical grains with a cement phase in the necks (Mars duricrust); gas pressure, bond fraction, cement conductivity varied.
## Findings
- Bond fractions <0.001–0.01 vol% small effect; 0.01–1% raise conductivity ×3–8; 1–15% further +25–100%; >15% approaches bedrock (abstract).
- Notes that vapor-deposited ice preferentially fills intergrain regions (sintering) (p. ~3).
