# braunRigorousApproachSpecific2024 — Braun, Fourteau & Löwe, The Cryosphere 18, 1653–1668 (2024)
DOI: 10.5194/tc-18-1653-2024. Read (16 pp; stochastic-uncertainty appendix skimmed).

## Summary
Exact SSA-evolution equation fed by pore-scale growth velocities from steady heat + vapor diffusion solved on µCT images of snow under TGM (two Snowbreeder series: −8.1 °C, 47 K m⁻¹, 384 h; −7.6 °C, 55 K m⁻¹, 665 h); α fitted by matching measured SSA.

## Findings
- Fitted α: 10⁻¹ then 10⁻¹·⁵ (series 1), 10⁻²·²⁵ (series 2); "lie in the commonly found range of 10⁻³ < α < 10⁻¹ (Libbrecht and Rickerby, 2013), which is also used by Kaempfer and Plapp (2009)" (p. ~13).
- "Using a single, constant value of α ... must be therefore understood as an effective kinetic coefficient" (p. ~13).
- SSA here is per volume (interface area normalized per volume) (p.1).

## Claims
- Draft L273 range α ∈ [10⁻³, 10⁻¹]: supported via Braun (who cite Libbrecht & Rickerby 2013 and K&P 2009).
- Draft L276 "modeled the steady state thermal and vapor diffusion ... In both these works, it was required to tune α for a given temperature": partially; outline L469–490 description and suggested text verified (temperatures, fitted values, "effective kinetic coefficient").
- Draft L439 (α_c = 0.1 "upper end of the range found in the literature", citing Braun, Bouvet 2022): supported for Braun (upper bound 10⁻¹, and their series 1 fitted 10⁻¹).
