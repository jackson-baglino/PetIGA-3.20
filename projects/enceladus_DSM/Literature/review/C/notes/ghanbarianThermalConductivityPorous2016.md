# ghanbarianThermalConductivityPorous2016 — Ghanbarian & Daigle, Water Resour. Res. 52, 295–314 (2016)
Read (main sections; derivations skimmed).
## Summary
Percolation-based effective-medium approximation (P-EMA) for porosity- and saturation-dependent conductivity of rocks/soils: endmember conductivities, a scaling exponent and a percolation threshold; non-universal exponents (vs 0.76 for 3D conductor–superconductor).
## Findings
- Measurements are usually of apparent conductivity when moisture redistributes under a gradient (p.2).
