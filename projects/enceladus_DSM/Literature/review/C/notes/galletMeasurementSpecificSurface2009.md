# galletMeasurementSpecificSurface2009 — Gallet, Domine, Zender & Picard, The Cryosphere 3, 167–182 (2009)
Read (16 pp; appendix ray-tracing details skimmed).

## Summary
DUFISSS: hemispherical IR reflectance in an integrating sphere at 1310/1550 nm, calibrated against CH4 adsorption; field-portable, one SSA value per ~1 min.

## Findings
- SSA = S/M = 6/(ρice deff) (Eq. 1): SSA per unit mass, m² kg⁻¹.
- Accuracy 10% (1310 nm, SSA < 60 m² kg⁻¹) and 12% (1550 nm, SSA > 60) (abstract).
- Other SSA methods: CH4 adsorption (6% reproducibility, 3 h), stereology, X-ray tomography (insufficient resolution for SSA > 70 m² kg⁻¹) (p.2).
- "snow SSA changes with time because of snow metamorphism ... many aspects of its rate of change are not elucidated" (p.2).

## Claims
- Outline L562 / L1318–1320 (SSA measured by near-IR reflectance, gas adsorption and tomography): supported (p.1–2).
- Note: field SSA is per mass (m² kg⁻¹); if the paper's SSA is per volume (ice or total), give the conversion SSA_mass = SSA_ice-vol/ρice so a lab can apply the law.
