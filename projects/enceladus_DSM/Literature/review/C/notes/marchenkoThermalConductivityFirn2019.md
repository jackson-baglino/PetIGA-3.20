# marchenkoThermalConductivityFirn2019 — Marchenko, van Pelt, Pettersson, Pohjola & Reijmer, The Cryosphere 13, 1843–1859 (2019)
Read (main text).
## Summary
Firn conductivity at Lomonosovfonna (Svalbard) reconstructed from subsurface temperatures by optimization: k* 0.2–1.6 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹, increasing downwards and over time, consistently above density-based parameterizations.
## Claims / additions
- Not cited. Supports draft L256 (firn conductivity controls glacier subsurface temperature, refreezing, runoff) and that density-only parameterizations can underestimate k. Optional addition alongside Oster & Albert (2022).
