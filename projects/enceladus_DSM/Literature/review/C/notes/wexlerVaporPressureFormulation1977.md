# wexlerVaporPressureFormulation1977 — Wexler, J. Res. Natl. Bur. Stand. 81A(1), 5–20 (1977)
No DOI in PDF. Read in full (text layer is OCR; Eqs. 53–54 checked on rendered PDF p.7, printed 11). Remaining pages are vapor-pressure tables and references.

## Summary
Thermodynamic formulation (Clausius–Clapeyron integrated with virial equation of state, ice specific volume and enthalpy, ideal-gas enthalpy of vapor) of the saturation vapor pressure over ice from the triple point (611.657 Pa at 273.16 K) to −100 °C; uncertainty 0.016% at the triple point to 0.5% at −100 °C; tables at 0.1 K steps.

## Findings relevant to the manuscript
- Final form (Eq. 54, rendered p.7/11): ln p = Σ_{j=0}^{4} K_j T^{j−1} + K_5 ln T, with T in K and p in Pa; coefficients in Table 2 (K0 = −0.58653696e4, K1 = 0.2224103300e2, K2 = 0.13749042e−1, K3 = −0.34031775e−4, K4 = 0.26967687e−7, K5 = 0.6918651e0 — same as Flatau Table 1). Eq. 54 is "the preferred equation because it has a rational thermodynamic basis"; Eq. 63 is a simpler empirical alternative (p.12).
- Vapor pressure 611.154 Pa at 0 °C (p.10); e.g., table: 103.276 Pa at −20 °C (Table 3, p.7) — matches the outline's "P_v ... 103 Pa at −20 °C" (from Murphy & Koop; consistent).
- Valid to −100 °C; covers −40 to −5 °C.
