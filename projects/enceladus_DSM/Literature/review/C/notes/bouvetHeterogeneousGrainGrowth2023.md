# bouvetHeterogeneousGrainGrowth2023 — Bouvet, Calonne, Flin & Geindreau, The Cryosphere 17, 3553–3573 (2023)
DOI (PDF p.1): 10.5194/tc-17-3553-2023. Read in full (21 pp; references skimmed).

## Summary
Two cold-lab experiments on snow layers under ~100 K m⁻¹ (−15.6/−3.1 °C plates, 210 kg m⁻³, 20 d; −14.5/−3.5 °C, 287 kg m⁻³, 28 d), static sampling + µCT (21, 8, 10 µm), temperature and humidity profiles. Results: heterogeneous grain growth (coarser depth hoar mid-layer, covariance length +50%), basal air gap >3 mm after a month, hard depth hoar with SSA increasing in the denser layer.

## Findings
- Purely experimental; no model, no condensation coefficient, no time rescaling (grep and full read).
- Depth hoar / faceted crystals "constitute typical weak layers involved in slab avalanches (Schweizer et al., 2003)" (p.1–2).
- SSA can increase under strong TGM in dense snow (hard depth hoar, p.8–9) — SSA is not monotone in all regimes.
