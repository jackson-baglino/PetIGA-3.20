# auriault2009_homogenization_book — Auriault, Boutin & Geindreau, Homogenization of Coupled Phenomena in Heterogenous Media, ISTE/Wiley (2009)
DOI (outline L1706): 10.1002/9780470612033. 465 pp. Read closely: Ch. 1 bilaminate (1.2), Ch. 2.5 (periodicity, REV), Ch. 4 (heat transfer, 4.1–4.3), Ch. 6.2 (effective conductivity of periodic media). Remaining chapters (porous-media flow, Klinkenberg, poroelasticity, waves; Ch. 5, 7–14) were skimmed by section heading and introduction only — they do not bear on any manuscript claim.

## Findings
- Cell problem notation: the periodic corrector is the vector **t** (t*_i solving the canonical problem P(λa, λb), Eqs. 4.26–4.30), with zero cell mean; λeff = ⟨λ (I + grad_y t)⟩ (Eq. 4.82) (pp. ~115–119). Supports the outline's convention "the vector t named as in Auriault et al." (L117).
- Classical macroscopic conduction holds for conductivities "of the same order of magnitude" (Sect. 4.2.2); high-contrast cases give memory effects only in the transient regime (weakly conducting connected phase, Sect. 4.2.3) or the infinitely-conducting-inclusion limit (Sect. 4.2.4). "A result of the separation of length scales is that the presence of transient effects does not have any effect on the effective coefficients" (Sect. 4.2.2.2). λeff symmetric positive definite.
- Periodicity: "the assumption of periodicity is not an obstacle in estimating the effective properties of a material with a random microstructure"; for random media the REV "is necessarily approximate" and depends on the property and on the phase contrast (Sect. 2.5.1, pp. 69–70).
- Ch. 6.2: SC/BCC sphere lattices, contrasts 10 and 100: λeff between Voigt/Reuss and close to HS+ bound; arrangement matters when inclusion fraction > 0.5; λeff highly sensitive to contrast for 10⁻³ < λa/λb < 10³.
- Sect. 4.3: imperfect contact (Biot condition, contact resistance 1/h) changes the macroscopic model depending on its magnitude — the physical analogue of the diffuse-interface Kapitza resistance Nicoli et al. remove.

## Claims
- Outline L117, L1706–1707 (book the paper follows for t; DOI 10.1002/9780470612033; not in .bib): supported (book uses t; DOI cannot be verified from the PDF text available — take from Crossref as the outline says).
- Outline L747–749 ("periodicity is an assumption, not a convenience"): supported in spirit — the book treats periodicity as an assumption of the method, and justifies applying periodic cells to random media as an approximation whose REV is property- and contrast-dependent (Sect. 2.5.1).
- Draft L353–362 (homogenization, separation of scales, cell problem): supported.
- Project convention "k_eff is steady-state only": consistent with Sect. 4.2.2.2 (effective coefficients unaffected by transients when contrasts are O(1)); for ice/air (contrast ~100) the steady keff is still the standard result.
