# osterThermalConductivityPolar2022 — Oster & Albert, J. Glaciol. 68(272), 1141–1148 (2022)
Read (8 pp).
## Summary
First direct guarded-hot-plate measurements of firn conductivity to 48 m depth (South Pole): k_firn(ρ) = 0.144 e^{0.00308 ρ}; k_firn(z) = 0.536 e^{0.0144 z}.
## Claims / additions
- Not cited. Could support draft L256 ("effective thermal conductivity of snowpacks and firn determines how heat moves ... affecting the stability of glacial ice") — Oster: "Both ice-sheet modeling and decoding satellite data require knowledge of the thermal conductivity of polar firn" (abstract). A better match for the firn/glacier part than Calonne 2011. Optional addition.
