# bouvetMultiscaleModelingHeat2024 — Bouvet, Calonne, Flin & Geindreau, The Cryosphere 18, 4285–4313 (2024)
Read (29 pp; upscaling derivations skimmed).
## Summary
Homogenization (Auriault et al. 2009) of heat + vapor transport with sublimation–deposition for α from 10⁻¹⁰ to 1; macroscopic models by α regime; compared with three TGM experiments and pore-scale simulations.
## Findings
- Transition αT = Dv/(l w_kc) ≈ 3×10⁻⁴ for l ≈ 0.5 mm; "values range between 10⁻³ for small grains (∼0.1 mm) and 10⁻⁵ for very large grains (∼5 mm)"; vapor transport limited by sublimation–deposition below αT and by diffusion above (abstract; Sect. 3).
- keff depends on ki, ka and microstructure; ki, ka temperature dependent (Huang et al.) (Sect. 4).
