# postberg_sodium_2009 — Postberg et al., Nature 459, 1098–1101 (2009)
DOI (PDF p.1): 10.1038/nature08046. Read in full (4 pp). SI not in library.

## Summary
Cassini CDA impact-ionization mass spectra of E-ring ice grains (mostly radii 0.1–1 µm), Oct 2004–Dec 2005, compared with laboratory laser-dispersion spectra of salt solutions. Identifies a Na-rich population (type III, ~6% of spectra) with 0.5–2% salts by mass (NaCl, NaHCO3/Na2CO3), implying a liquid-water (ocean) source.

## Findings relevant to the manuscript
- Grain sizes: CDA spectra "of E-ring particles, predominantly with radii of 0.1–1 µm" (p.1/1098).
- Bulk water ice: "Previous CDA results show that the bulk material of E-ring particles is water ice" (p.1/1098).
- Salt content: "About 6% of these spectra (hereafter 'type III' spectra) show ... ice particles with particularly high sodium contents ... sodium salts NaCl, NaHCO3 and/or Na2CO3"; abstract text: salts "(0.5–2% by mass)" (p.1/1098). Type I/II (the majority, >90%) have Na/H2O ≈ 5e-8 to 5e-5 (Fig. 1 caption, p.2/1099) — trace levels.
- Measured grains are E-ring grains (fed by the plume), not grains sampled in the plume itself.
- "most of the total ejected grain mass falls back to the moon's surface (the cryo-volcanically active regions should be covered with Na-bearing plume particles)" (p.3/1100).

## Claims supported / contradicted / qualified
- Draft L259 / outline L314–316 "The ice grains that form this plume are micron-sized, composed primarily of water ice, and contain percent level NaCl (Postberg 2009)": partially supported. Micron/sub-micron and water-ice dominated: yes. "Percent-level" salts: only for the ~6% Na-rich population (0.5–2% by mass, of which NaCl is the main salt); the majority carry only traces. Suggest "and some contain percent-level salts, mostly NaCl (Postberg et al., 2009)". (Choukroun 2020 uses the same "percent-level NaCl" phrasing, citing Postberg 2009, 2011.)
