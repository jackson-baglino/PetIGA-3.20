# millerMicrostructuralApproachPredict2003 — Miller, Adams & Brown, Cold Reg. Sci. Technol. 37, 213–226 (2003)
Read (14 pp).
## Summary
Grain–bond (neck) model unifying isothermal and gradient metamorphism with heat and vapor transport; sintering from vapor diffusion with latent heat.
## Findings
- Hobbs & Mason (1964) "very meticulously" showed vapor diffusion as the dominant isothermal sintering mechanism; their observed and computed transfer coefficients "agreed to a factor of five" (p. ~4).
- Snow "sinters very rapidly initially, then decreases dramatically in time" (p. ~4).

- Not cited. Prior grain–neck metamorphism model; in-library secondary source on Hobbs & Mason. Optional for Intro para 4.
