# mergnyLunaIcyExploringEuropas2024 — Mergny, Schmidt, et al., PSJ 5:216 (2024)
DOI (PDF p.1): 10.3847/PSJ/ad6d6d. Read (14 pp).
## Summary
LunaIcy: grain/bond vapor-transport sintering model coupled to the MultIHeaTS heat solver with bond-dependent porous conductivity (two-way coupling); Europa over 1 Myr; effective sintering where daily T briefly exceeds 115 K.
## Findings
- At low T on icy moons the dominant mechanism is vapor transport (Kossacki 1994; Blackford 2007; Gundlach 2018; Molaro 2019) (p.3).
- Validation against Hobbs & Mason (1964): spheres 50–700 µm, −3 to −20 °C, atmospheric pressure; with α = 0.03 the model reproduces bond growth at −3 °C (Fig. 3); fitted α "approximately 17 times smaller than expected" because diffusion through air at 1 atm reduces ΔP near surfaces (p. ~6).
- "sintering drastically changes the thermal conductivity of ice. As bond growth occurs, the surface area of solid to solid increases, leading to an increase in conductivity" (p. ~9); conductivity expression from bond radius; k0 = 567/T.
