# matzlMeasuringSpecificSurface2006 — Matzl & Schneebeli (2006), J. Glaciol. 52(179), 558–564

**Summary.** Calibrated near-infrared (840–940 nm) digital photography of snow-pit walls; reflectance correlated with SSA measured by stereology on cast surface sections of 29 samples; exponential calibration SSA = A·exp(r/t), R² = 90.8 %; 2-D SSA maps at ~1 mm resolution with ~15 % uncertainty.

**Findings relevant to the manuscript**
- p.1 (558) abstract: "Calibrated NIR photography allows quantitative determination of SSA and its spatial variation in a snow profile in two dimensions within an uncertainty of 15%".
- SSA definition p.1: "SSA (mm⁻¹) is defined as the ratio between surface area and the volume of the ice phase."
- SSA "is an essential microstructural parameter for the characterization of sintered materials such as snow (German, 1996)" (p.1); "The SSA of snow changes during isothermal and temperature-gradient metamorphism (Schneebeli and Sokratov, 2004; Legagneux and Domine, 2005)".
- p.5 (562): "Because of the widespread use of SSA to parameterize air permeability and thermal conductivity of sintered materials (German, 1996), it can be expected that this should also be possible for snow."
- SSA vs density only loosely correlated (Fig. 4, p.5): "For densities from approximately 150 to 300 kg m⁻³ a wider range of SSA values can be observed."
- Grain-size effect dominates NIR "as long as the snow density is < 650 kg m⁻³" (p.1, citing Dozier 1992).

**Claims supported / qualified**
- Outline L561–564 and §5.4 cite box ("SSA is measured in the field and laboratory ... Matzl & Schneebeli 2006"): **supported** (field method, 15 % uncertainty).
- Outline §5.3 "SSA as the state variable": Matzl & Schneebeli p.5 is a closely matching statement that SSA is used to parameterize the thermal conductivity of sintered materials (via German 1996) and expected to work for snow — direct support for the paper's motivation to relate k_eff to SSA. Quote it as their expectation, not a demonstration.

**Candidate additions.** Add Matzl & Schneebeli (2006, p.5) to §5.3 ¶7/Intro "Why SSA": "SSA is used to parameterize the thermal conductivity of sintered materials, and Matzl & Schneebeli (2006) anticipated the same for snow" — this paper tests that expectation for isothermal sintering.
