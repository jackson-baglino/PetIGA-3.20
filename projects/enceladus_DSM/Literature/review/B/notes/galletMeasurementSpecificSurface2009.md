# galletMeasurementSpecificSurface2009 — Gallet, Domine, Zender & Picard (2009), The Cryosphere 3, 167–182

**Summary.** DUFISSS instrument: hemispherical infrared reflectance of snow samples in a 15 cm integrating sphere at 1310 nm (SSA < 60 m²/kg) or 1550 nm (higher SSA), calibrated against CH4 adsorption; DISORT/ray-tracing modelling of density and geometry artifacts; field deployment.

**Findings relevant to the manuscript**
- Abstract: "For SSA<60 m² kg⁻¹, we use the 1310 nm radiation ... the accuracy of SSA determination is 10%"; one SSA value "in about one minute, and it successfully operates outdoors in polar conditions".
- Other SSA methods listed: CH4 adsorption (3 h, reproducibility 6 %), stereology, X-ray tomography (p.2).
- SSA defined per mass (m²/kg) here (vs per ice volume in Proksch/Matzl).

**Claims supported / qualified**
- Outline L561–564 and §5.4 cite box (SSA measured in field and laboratory; Gallet 2009): **supported**.
- Units: per-mass SSA (m²/kg) — the manuscript must state which SSA definition it uses (see Proksch notes).

**Candidate additions.** None.
