# johnsonMicrostructureIcyLunar2025 — Johnson, Asenath-Smith, Lieblappen, Dreyer, Cannon, Shope & Jehn (2025), J. Phys. Chem. C 129, 2152–2164

**Summary.** Micro-CT comparison of icy lunar-regolith simulants made four ways: pressure-sintered (PSS), temperature-sintered (TSS, new here), wet-mixed, and unconsolidated granular. Wet mixing (liquid water) produces microstructures very different from the lunar-relevant solid-state sintered samples (porosity, pore size, ice particle size, ice–regolith interconnection). Not cited in this manuscript (relevant to the sister lunar project).

**Findings relevant**
- Abstract: "The lunar near subsurface cannot support liquid water due to very low temperatures and pressures, so water ice modification processes are limited to sublimation deposition."
- Intro: "icy regolith simulant bulk properties may be varied by sintering the samples in the solid phase", citing their earlier PSS work (ref 13 = Asenath-Smith 2025, which is missing from the library).
- Discussion: quotes Molaro-type estimates, e.g. neck formation by sublimation–deposition alone in ~30,000 years, and at 80 K none within 4.6 Ga (refs 65–66).
