# stehleMigrationBubblesIce1967 — Stehle (1967), Physics of Snow and Ice (Proc. Int. Conf. Low Temp. Sci., Sapporo 1966), HUSCAP copy

**Summary.** Laboratory measurements of how fast vapour figures and air bubbles migrate in ice under a temperature gradient, compared with a modified version of Nakaya's formula, which accounts for internal air pressure and figure shape. Applications are bubble foliation in glaciers and brine-pocket migration in sea ice.

**Relevance.** Not cited, and not relevant to the manuscript beyond the general principle that vapour is transported from warm to cold ice surfaces (p.3: "ice sublimates from the warm side of the figure and condenses on the cold side"). Read only to the extent needed to establish scope (the abstract, introduction and methods; the later results pages were skimmed). No claims affected.
