# srivastavaObservationTemperatureGradient2010 — Srivastava, Mahajan, Satyawali & Kumar (2010), Ann. Glaciol. 51(54), 73–82, doi:10.3189/172756410791386571

**Summary.** Fine-grained Himalayan snow was sieved into a heat-flux apparatus (~96 K/m, cold room −12 °C, base −2 °C) for 6 days and sampled by µCT. Measured: density, SSA, ice and pore thickness, SMI and MIL fabric. Linear elastic moduli computed by voxel FE. Not cited in the manuscript.

**Findings relevant**
- p.1: "The effective properties of snow, including mechanical properties, depend not only on the density but also on the morphology of ice and pore phase (Schweizer and others, 2003; Kaempfer and others, 2005)."
- Abstract: density rose ~14 % on day 1 then flattened; SSA fell 22 %; computed Young's moduli are 1.5–4× published dynamic values.
- p.1: depth hoar forms are weak layers because of "large grain size and reduced degree of inter-granular bonding".

**Relevance.** Optional secondary support for "density is not enough" (mechanical, not thermal) and for draft L256. Calonne 2014 is the better thermal citation. No changes needed.
