# maMicrostructuralEvolutionMechanical2025 — Ma et al. (2025), Construction and Building Materials (Elsevier), snow under triaxial compression with CT

**Summary.** Triaxial compression tests on snow (for polar runways) with CT at loading stages. A curvature- and skeleton-based grain segmentation quantifies the number and area of ice bonds; density and loading rate are varied. Not cited.

**Findings relevant.** Abstract: during hardening "the ice bond area decreases significantly, leading to a lower tangent modulus. Horizontal ice bonds break more easily than vertical ones". Intro: "ice bonds gradually form between snow grains, binding them together into a complex microstructure. This microstructure determines the mechanical properties of snow."

**Relevance.** Mechanical analogue of bond control. Not needed for a conductivity paper. No citation recommended. Read to establish scope (abstract, introduction).
