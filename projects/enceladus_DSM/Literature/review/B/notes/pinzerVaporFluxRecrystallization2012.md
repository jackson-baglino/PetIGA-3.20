# pinzerVaporFluxRecrystallization2012 — Pinzer, Schneebeli & Kaempfer (2012), The Cryosphere 6, 1141–1155, doi:10.5194/tc-6-1141-2012

**Summary.** In-situ time-lapse µCT of snow under a steady 50 K/m gradient. Sublimation and deposition locations are tracked directly, the ice residence time is computed, and vapour flux is compared with hand-to-hand transport. Not cited in the manuscript.

**Findings relevant**
- Abstract p.1: "a characteristic residence time of 2–3 days"; "a mass turnover of up to 60 % of the total ice mass per day"; "we did not find evidence for a macroscopic vapor diffusion enhancement."
- p.1: "The high homologous temperature, combined with the particularly high vapor pressure of water, can cause rapid sintering and large structural modifications by vapor diffusion."
- p.2: snow's thermal-insulation role "is related to the low thermal conductivity of snow (Sturm et al., 1997; Calonne et al., 2011)".

**Relevance.** This is a temperature-gradient study and outside our isothermal scope. The p.1 sentence could serve as background for "ice is unusually volatile near its melting point" (draft L250). Optional.
