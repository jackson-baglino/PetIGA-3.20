# shimizuImpactComplexTopology2017 — Shimizu & Tanaka (2017), Sci. Adv. 3, eaap9570

**Summary.** A phase-field model with two coupled order parameters (binary-mixture composition and porous-matrix density) for phase separation inside random bicontinuous porous media (frozen spinodal structures, Vycor-like), in 2-D and 3-D, under neutral, partial and perfect wetting. Not cited in the manuscript.

**Findings relevant**
- p.4 (4 of 9): "there is a crucial difference in the pattern formed in the late stage between 2D and 3D. In 2D, a network structure is immediately destroyed ... In 3D, on the other hand, bicontinuous double networks of the two phases can be formed ... there is an essential effect of the dimensionality of a pore structure on the connectivity". The reason given is that in 2-D a bicontinuous porous structure is impossible.
- p.5: coarsening k1 ∝ t^−1/3 in the wetting-film regime, with evaporation–condensation lengthening plugs in 2-D.

**Relevance.** This is a closely matching general point for the outline's "Why periodic, why two dimensions" paragraph: in 2-D, two phases cannot both percolate in the same direction, whereas in 3-D they can. Our 2-D ice networks therefore have connectivity (and conduction paths through air) that differ in kind from 3-D packings, which limits quantitative transfer of k_eff to 3-D. Cite it, together with Wang & Baker 2017 p.3, only for that topological statement.
