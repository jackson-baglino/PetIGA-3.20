# libbrechtGrowthRatesPrincipal2003 — Libbrecht (2003), J. Cryst. Growth 247, 530–540 (minus signs dropped in the text layer: the range is −10 to −40 °C)

**Summary.** Interferometric growth rates of basal and prism facets of small ice crystals on a substrate, grown from vapour at −10 to −40 °C as a function of supersaturation. Data fit 2-D-nucleation-limited growth; critical supersaturations are similar for both facets and fall as T rises. Facet differences are attributed to surface diffusion of admolecules. Not cited.

**Findings relevant.** Abstract: "facet growth is limited primarily by 2D nucleation over this temperature range"; "much of the peculiar behavior seen in ice crystal growth from vapor is due to differences in the surface diffusion of admolecules on the two principal facets". p.2: edges between facets "are expected to be atomically rough, and admolecules are thought to be readily incorporated onto rough surfaces".

**Relevance.** Background for why α depends on supersaturation and facet. It is superseded for our purposes by Libbrecht & Rickerby 2013 (already noted). No citation needed.
