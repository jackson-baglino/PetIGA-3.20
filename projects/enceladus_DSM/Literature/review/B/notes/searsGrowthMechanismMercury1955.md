# searsGrowthMechanismMercury1955 — Sears (1955), Acta Metall. 3, 361–366 (title: "A growth mechanism for mercury whiskers")

**Summary.** Mercury whiskers (~0.01 µm radius, 1–2 mm long) grown by condensing vapour on glass at −63.5 °C. Each whisker carries an axial screw dislocation. Adsorbed atoms diffuse over the surface to the growing tip ("growth rate ... a thousandfold greater than could be accounted for by direct impingement").

**Relevance.** Not cited. The only connection is general: surface diffusion of adsorbed molecules can supply growth faster than direct impingement, which is background on why attachment kinetics are not captured by a single α. No manuscript claim depends on it. Read to establish scope (abstract, introduction and experimental section).
