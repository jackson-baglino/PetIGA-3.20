# millerMicrostructuralApproachPredict2003 — Miller, Adams & Brown (2003), Cold Reg. Sci. Technol. 37, 213–226, doi:10.1016/j.coldregions.2003.07.001 (printed)

**Summary.** A network model of grains and necks (spheres joined by concave necks, on a regular 2-D/axisymmetric lattice) that couples heat conduction through the ice network, vapour diffusion in the pore, and phase change at the surfaces, unified across isothermal and gradient conditions. Includes isothermal parameter studies of bond/grain ratio, grain size, density and temperature, and the transition to kinetic growth. Not cited in the manuscript.

**Findings relevant**
- p.2 (214): "In the absence of a macroscopic temperature gradient, ice particles are observed to sinter by growing the bonds between the individual grains." Colbeck's point that true equi-temperature metamorphism is impossible.
- p.7 (219): isothermal bond growth slows by two orders of magnitude as r_b/r_g rises from 0.2 to 0.6; "The bond growth rate decreases by a factor of approximately 8" for the temperature range studied (Fig. 5).
- p.6: "the interconnecting neck size has recently been recognized as an equally important" parameter as grain size.
- p.4: heat conduction is through the ice network only, because "the thermal conductivity of saturated vapor ... is two orders of magnitude smaller than ... pure ice". Air conduction is neglected, which Calonne 2011 shows matters.

**Relevance.** It is a precedent for an explicit grain–neck sintering model with heat and vapour in snow, and could be added to the "earlier models" list (draft L276) as the network-model counterpart to K&P. Optional.
