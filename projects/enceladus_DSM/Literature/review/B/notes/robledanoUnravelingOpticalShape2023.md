# robledanoUnravelingOpticalShape2023 — Robledano, Picard, Dumont, Flin, Arnaud & Libois (2023), Nat. Commun., doi:10.1038/s41467-023-39671-3 (printed)

**Summary.** Ray tracing through 3-D µCT images of natural snow yields the "optical shape" parameters B (absorption enhancement) and g_G (geometric asymmetry). Snow optically behaves like convex, asymmetric particles rather than spheres, which cuts the related climate-model temperature uncertainty by a factor of 3. Not cited in the manuscript.

**Findings relevant.** p.1: once deposited, crystals "establish bonds with each other and their shape drastically changes ... making snow cover a two-phase porous material made of ice and air, rather than a collection of individual particles"; metamorphism leads to varied microstructure.

**Relevance.** Only general: microstructure controls a bulk property (albedo) beyond what an equivalent-sphere size captures. It is the optical analogue of our argument, but not closely matching for conductivity. No citation recommended. Read to establish scope (introduction and results summary).
