# krishnamachariGibbsThomsonFormulaSmall1996 — Krishnamachari, McLean, Cooper & Sethna (1996), Phys. Rev. B 54, 8899–8907 (library copy is the arXiv preprint, regenerated 2018, with a different title wording)

**Summary.** Lattice-gas Monte Carlo of 2-D islands. The classical Gibbs–Thomson relation (ideal-gas vapour) fails at high vapour densities; corrections come from the Ising-mapped equation of state. Also covers finite-size stability and the microscopic origin of the Gibbs–Thomson effect (higher detachment per unit edge for small islands). Not cited.

**Relevance.** None. Water vapour over ice at −5 to −40 °C is a dilute ideal gas, so the classical (linearized) Gibbs–Thomson relation used in the manuscript is appropriate. No citation recommended.
