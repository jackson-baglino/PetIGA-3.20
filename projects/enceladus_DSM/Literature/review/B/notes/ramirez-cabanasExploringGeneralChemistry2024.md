# ramirez-cabanasExploringGeneralChemistry2024 — Ramírez-Cabañas, Flandes & Mirón-Enríquez (2024), Adv. Space Res. (COSPAR), Enceladus core and ocean chemistry

**Summary.** Equilibrium speciation (PhreeqC) of a four-layer Enceladus with three core scenarios (ordinary chondrite, carbonaceous chondrite plus igneous inclusion, peridotite) reacting with a primordial ocean, compared with plume species (NaCl, NaHCO₃/Na₂CO₃, SiO₂, phosphates). Not cited in the manuscript.

**Findings relevant.** p.2: lists CDA-detected grain components (H₂O, NaCl, NaOH, NaHCO₃, Na₂CO₃, SiO₂) citing Postberg 2009–2023; south polar power ~5 GW.

**Relevance.** None for the manuscript, which treats pure ice; Postberg 2009/2018 already cover grain composition. No citation recommended. Read to establish scope.
