# mcdowellColdLaboratoryHyperspectral2024 — McDowell, Keegan, Skiles, Donahue, Osterberg, Hawley & Marshall (2024), The Cryosphere 18, 1925–1946, doi:10.5194/tc-18-1925-2024

**Summary.** A cold-lab NIR hyperspectral imager (900–1700 nm) maps effective grain size and ice layers in 14 Greenland firn cores at 0.4 mm resolution. Effective grain sizes are ~1.5× visual radii. Tracks the 2012 melt event. Not cited.

**Findings relevant.** In the discussion: "sintering processes further reduce total surface area as firn grains become bonded together" (low-density firn 350–550 kg/m³). This explains part of the optical-versus-visual size difference.

**Relevance.** Minimal: it is a SSA-type (optical) measurement of firn. No citation recommended. Read to establish scope (abstract, methods summary, the discussion paragraph above).
