# scanlanNonlinearRadarResponse2022 — Scanlan, Rutishauser, Young & Blankenship (2022), Icarus (Europa plume fallout; "Non-linear radar response to the radial structure of Europa plume" deposits)

**Summary.** Models how layered plume-fallout deposits around Europa vents (with deposition from Quick & Hedman 2020) change the coherent surface reflectivity seen by REASON and RIME radar sounders, for plume heights and durations. Deposit porosity is 0.9 for fresh deposits and 0.5 for "progressively densified" deposits. Not cited in the manuscript.

**Findings relevant**
- Abstract p.1: reflectivity oscillation amplitude depends on porosity, "with greater oscillation amplitudes being associated with older fallout deposits that have undergone some degree of densification"; "there is no direct proportionality between observed radar surface reflectivity and near-surface bulk density."
- p.3: porosity 0.5 represents "a plume deposit that has been progressively densified over time and 0.9 ... freshly fallen snow".

**Relevance.** Europa is outside the manuscript's framing, and porosity, not conductivity, is what matters here. At most it could be a one-clause pointer that the evolution of plume-deposit microstructure matters for radar interpretation, which Byrne 2024 already covers for Enceladus. No citation recommended.
