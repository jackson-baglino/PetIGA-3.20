# karmaPhasefieldMethodComputationally1996 — Karma & Rappel (1996), Phys. Rev. E 53(4), R3017–R3020

**Summary.** The short communication that introduces the thin-interface analysis, giving a Gibbs–Thomson condition with arbitrary (including zero) kinetic coefficient at a W/d0 that can be resolved. Tested on 2-D dendrites at zero kinetics against Green's-function solutions ("within a few percent"). Not cited.

**Relevance.** The precursor of Karma & Rappel 1998. Either can be cited for the thin-interface correction; the 1998 paper gives the constants. No separate citation needed.
