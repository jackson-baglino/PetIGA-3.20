# libbrechtComprehensiveModelSnow2014 — Libbrecht (2014), arXiv:1407.0740 (as in the bib), on ice growth near the melting point and step energies

**Summary.** Near the melting point, premelting gives a quasi-liquid layer. Growth from vapour (basal σ0 ≈ 0.4 % at −2 °C; prism σ0 < 0.01 %) and from liquid water both show nucleation barriers with about the same molecular step energy, implying a common nucleation picture. Not cited.

**Findings relevant.** p.1: "vn ∼ exp(−σ0/σ)"; "at T = −2 C, values of σ0 ≈ 0.4 percent on the basal surface and σ0 < 0.01 percent on the prism surface".

**Relevance.** With σ0 ≈ 0.4 % on the basal facet and sintering supersaturations of ~10⁻⁵, faceted basal growth would be strongly suppressed. This reinforces (with Libbrecht & Rickerby 2013) that a constant α_c is an effective parameter. No new citation needed.
