# mcdowellFirnCoreEvidence2023 — McDowell, Keegan, Wever, Osterberg, Hawley & Marshall (2023), JGR Earth Surface 128, e2022JF006752

**Summary.** Grain-size and ice-layer stratigraphy from seven western Greenland percolation-zone firn cores (GreenTrACS 2016), plus SNOWPACK simulations of meltwater at grain-size transitions. Transitions are anticorrelated with melt proxies and ice-layer counts, because rapid wet grain growth erases them. Not cited.

**Relevance.** This is wet-firn hydrology. The outline removed firn aquifers from the Intro (outline L140–145), so the paper is irrelevant to the current manuscript. Read to establish scope (abstract, introduction, conclusions). No citation recommended.
