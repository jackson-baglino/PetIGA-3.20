# postberg_macromolecular_2018 — Postberg et al. (2018), Nature 558, 564–568 (+ Methods, Extended Data)

**Summary.** Cassini CDA impact mass spectra of individual ice grains in the plume and E ring (2004–2008) plus INMS fast-vs-slow flyby spectra. A subgroup of organic-bearing grains shows high-mass organic cation (HMOC) series indicating macromolecular organics >200 u (aromatic + aliphatic, O/N-bearing); proposed formation from an organic film on the ocean surface via bubble bursting.

**Findings relevant to the manuscript**
- p.1 (564): "about 25% of the ice grain spectra of the E ring, the so-called type-2 spectra, exhibit the presence of organic material"; a subgroup (~3 % of type-2) shows HMOCs.
- p.2 (565): "Ion abundances are indicative of an organic fraction in ice grains up to the per cent level ... Particle radii are mostly between 0.2 µm and 2 µm"; "an ice–organic mixture constitutes the bulk composition of these particles".
- Methods (text file l.123 of remainder, PDF p.~6): "the actual fraction of ice grains carrying substantial amounts of complex organic material ejected into the E ring is at least 4% of the grains in the size range covered by the CDA (grain radius r ≈ 0.2–2 µm)".
- HMOC grains detected in the plume as well as the E ring (Methods).

**Claims supported / qualified**
- Draft L259 / outline L315–316 "...and complex organic material (Postberg et al., 2018)": **supported**, with the qualifier that a minority of grains (~4 % carry complex macromolecular organics; ~25 % carry some organics) — "some grains contain complex organic material" is the accurate phrasing. Grain radii 0.2–2 µm support "micron-sized".
